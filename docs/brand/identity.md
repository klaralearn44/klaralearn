# KlaraLearn Brand Identity

## Logo

File: `docs/brand/klara-learn-logo-sketch.png`

**Logomark:** Stylised "K" letterform with an open book at its base and an orange accent dot at the vertex of the K.  
**Logotype:** "Klara" (dark navy) + "Learn" (teal/emerald) — two-tone wordmark, no space between words.  
**Tagline:** "Global tutors. Brighter futures."

## Brand colours (extracted from logo sketch)

| Role | Hex (approx) | Usage |

## Voice & tone

- Reassuring and professional — parents are making an important decision for their child
- Affordable without feeling cheap — lead with quality, then price
- International but locally-relevant — tutors from around the world who understand UK exams
- Avoid jargon; parents scan quickly

## Key messaging

- "Affordable private tutors for 11 Plus, GCSE & SATs"
- "Professional-quality tutoring at significantly lower price points than the UK market average"
- "Global tutors. Brighter futures." (tagline)

## Target audience

- Primary: Parents of UK secondary school children (ages 10–16)
- Decision context: Choosing a private tutor for the 11 Plus, GCSE revision, SATs, or Common Entrance
- Pain points: UK tutor prices (£30–£80/hr), finding vetted/trustworthy tutors, unsure how to judge quality

## Competitive positioning

- vs MyTutor / Tutorful / First Tutors: lower price point, international tutor pool
- vs local agencies: platform transparency, online flexibility, ratings/reviews visible upfront
